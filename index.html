Promise Tracker NYC

Did the officials New York elected in 2025 do what they said they would? Ask about a promise, and a team of research agents checks it against the record.

What it explores

Campaign promises by Mayor Zohran Mamdani and the 51 City Council members, checked across three layers: what they promised (archived campaign pages), what they did (Council bills, votes, discretionary funding), and what happened (NYC Open Data, city spending, news).

Who it's for

New Yorkers deciding how they feel about their representatives, local reporters, and civic groups who want a cited, neutral check rather than a talking point.

How to use it

Name an official (or "the mayor", or a district) and a promise or topic. If your question is too broad, the agent asks you to narrow it. Every claim in the report links to its source; it says so when the data can't answer.
