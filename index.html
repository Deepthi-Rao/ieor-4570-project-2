What it explores

Promises by Mayor Zohran Mamdani and the City Council members in office since 2026, checked by a team of research agents across all three layers, with a verdict for each.

Who it's for

New Yorkers sizing up their representatives, local reporters, and civic groups who want a cited, neutral check, not a talking point.

How to use it

Name an official (or "the mayor", or a district) and a promise or topic. Too broad, and the agent asks you to narrow it. Every claim links to its source, and it says when the data can't answer.
